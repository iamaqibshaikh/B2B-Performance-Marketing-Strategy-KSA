# B2B Growth Strategy & Performance Marketing Roadmap
> **Market Focus:** Saudi Arabia (KSA) | **Industry:** Industrial Safety & Vocational Training | **Client/Entity:** Etihad Pioneers

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

This repository presents a comprehensive B2B Performance Marketing Proposal and Competitive Intelligence Analysis designed for the Saudi Arabian corporate training sector. The strategy targets enterprise decision-makers (HSE Directors, Procurement Heads, F&B Managers, and Fleet Supervisors) to generate high-intent corporate lead volume across five key training verticals: **IPAF**, **PASMA**, **Defensive Driving**, **Food Safety**, and **Hospitality Management**.

## 🎯 Target Audience & Buyer Personas

To prevent budget leakage on individual job seekers or B2C students, campaigns utilize strict negative keyword strategies and B2B audience signals targeting:

* **Safety & Operations:** HSE Directors, Fleet Supervisors, Scaffolding Managers (Focus: *IPAF, PASMA, Defensive Driving*).
* **Hospitality & Quality:** F&B Directors, Executive Chefs, QA Leads (Focus: *SFDA & HACCP Food Safety*).
* **Corporate & HR:** Hotel General Managers, L&D Directors (Focus: *Hospitality Guest Excellence*).

## 📈 Performance Governance & Optimization Framework

1. **Cost Per Lead (CPL) Trigger:** If CPL exceeds SAR 400 by Day 15, underperforming ad groups are paused and budget is reallocated to top-converting verticals.
2. **Quality Control Trigger:** If legitimate B2B RFQ rate falls below 50%, negative search terms and demographic filters are immediately tightened.
3. **Marketing-Sales Feedback Loop:** Daily CRM syncs ensure sales feedback on lead validity directly refines Google Ads bidding models.
